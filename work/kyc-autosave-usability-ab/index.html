KYC Autosave Flow Experiment (Usability A/B)

Compared a seamless KYC flow vs a review-first (autosave-centric) flow to validate clarity, confidence, and error rate.

Variants

Flow A — Seamless

Direct KTP → Selfie → confirmation without returning to a central review state.

Flow B — Review-first

Returns to Review after each photo step; Review acts as a progress “truth state”.

What we learned

  • No statistically significant difference overall, but Flow B trended better.
  • High-impact UX issues: ambiguous Edit/Delete affordances and unclear entry CTA language.
  • Autosave was positively perceived—especially for recovery after interruption.

Decision

Decision: Use Flow B as baseline and fix the error-prone UI affordances (Edit/Delete) and entry point clarity.

Monitoring plan

  • Step drop-offs (photo → review → form → OTP → submit)
  • Retake/edit interactions as friction proxies
  • Resume behavior after interruption
